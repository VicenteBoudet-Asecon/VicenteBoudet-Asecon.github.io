The Asecon Seal is our lifetime guarantee on the firm's work: if an assessment or a penalty
arises as a result of what we filed, we take care of it. No expiry date, and no argument
required from the client.

The scope is worth spelling out, because a guarantee nobody understands reassures nobody. It
covers errors attributable to our work: a return calculated incorrectly, a deadline we were
responsible for tracking, a criterion wrongly applied. It does not cover contingencies
arising from information the client did not give us, or gave us incompletely, nor
retroactive changes in legislation.

That distinction is what makes the guarantee sustainable. A firm that promises to answer for
everything, including what it does not control, either fails to honour it or prices it in.

If you have questions about how it applies in your case, let us discuss it before you engage
us: we would rather be explicit up front.
