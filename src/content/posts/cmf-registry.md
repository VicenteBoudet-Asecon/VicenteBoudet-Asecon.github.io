Chile's Financial Market Commission (CMF) maintains a public register of firms authorised to
audit the financial statements of entities under its supervision. Asecon has appeared on it
since 2016, under number 418.

The listing is not a formality. It requires demonstrating professional structure,
independence from audited parties, documented quality control procedures and staff with the
corresponding technical training. Registered firms are also subject to oversight by the
Commission itself.

For a company that needs its financial statements audited, the practical takeaway is simple:
check that the auditor is actually registered, and under what number. The register is public
and can be consulted directly on the CMF website.
